FAQ

Honest Answers

The questions every client asks — answered straight, before you spend anything.

How long does SEO take to show results?
SEO takes time. Early movement usually comes gradually as fixes, content and authority build up — and it depends on your market and competition. Anyone promising you the #1 spot in a week is selling you risk, not results. What I promise instead is honest work and clear reporting, so you can see progress as it happens.
Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?
Yes. I am based in Faisalabad, Pakistan and work remotely with clients worldwide — including real estate and e-commerce businesses in markets such as Dubai and the wider UAE. Everything runs over WhatsApp, email and video calls.
What do you need from me to start?
Just your website URL. I start with a free audit of your site, then share the findings and a clear roadmap. If you decide to go ahead, we agree the scope together before any paid work begins.
Do you guarantee rankings?
No — and you should be careful with anyone who does. No honest specialist can guarantee a specific ranking. What I do guarantee is honest, white-hat methods and transparent reporting on the work done and what comes next.
Which niches do you specialise in?
Real estate and e-commerce come first — agencies, portals, online stores and marketplace sellers are where AS Agency focuses. Other niches are welcome too; the audit will show whether I am the right fit for your site.
